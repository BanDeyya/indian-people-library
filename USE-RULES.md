# Use rules

The data licence ([LICENSE-DATA.md](LICENSE-DATA.md), CC BY 4.0) lets you reuse this library freely.
These rules are how we ask you to use it, and every contribution to this repository must follow them.

Please do not use the library, or images and text made from it:
1. to create sexual, nude or sexually suggestive content of any character;
2. to publish anything about a child character other than ordinary, fully clothed everyday scenes,
   or to publish child characters' reusable face sets;
3. to impersonate a real person, make deepfakes, or make a character look like a real, identifiable person;
4. to make people believe a character is a real person (fake profiles, fake reviews or testimonials, scams);
5. to demean, stereotype, harass or discriminate against any religion, community, caste, tribe,
   region, gender or other group, or to make propaganda against them;
6. to show communities this library leaves out on purpose (each region file's `excluded_communities`);
7. to make disinformation, such as fake news images of real events, conflicts or elections.

Label every image made with this library as AI-generated, and keep that label and any metadata.
