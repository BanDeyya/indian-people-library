    About

    Every person here is fictional. Each character has a fixed look, a wardrobe researched from India's own sources (state tourism and culture departments, census, museums, community sources), the festivals of their own community, and a portrait prompt you can paste into your own image model. The project does not make or host images.

    Children are never shown on this site. Equal respect for every region, religion and community: sacred, honour and marital items are used only where they belong, and no character carries a caste surname.

    Characters marked generated are drafts drawn from the reviewed, hand-made characters of the same community. If something is wrong or disrespectful, use Report a problem on that character; native reviews are the most useful help.
